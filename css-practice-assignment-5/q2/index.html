Overview

Kyoto is Japan's former imperial capital, known for its thousands of temples, traditional wooden houses, and quiet gardens tucked between busy streets.

Culture

From tea ceremonies to geisha districts, Kyoto preserves centuries of Japanese tradition in the middle of a modern city.

Food

Kaiseki dining, matcha sweets, and street food markets make Kyoto a destination for food lovers as much as history lovers.

Travel Tips

Visit in spring for cherry blossoms or autumn for fall colors, and rent a bicycle to explore the smaller side streets.
