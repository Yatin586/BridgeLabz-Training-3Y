The Daily Bulletin

Security researchers continue to track a rise in ransomware campaigns targeting small and medium businesses across the region. Analysts note that many of these attacks begin with a simple phishing email rather than a sophisticated exploit.

Once inside a network, attackers often move laterally for days or weeks before deploying encryption payloads, giving defenders a narrow window to detect unusual activity before serious damage occurs.

Experts recommend regular backups stored offline, prompt patching of known vulnerabilities, and continuous staff training as the most cost effective defenses available to smaller organizations.

Government agencies have also begun offering free assessment tools to help businesses identify weaknesses in their existing infrastructure before an incident occurs.

Industry groups expect the trend to continue through the year, urging companies of every size to treat cybersecurity as an ongoing operational priority rather than a one time project.
